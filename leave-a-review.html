Thank You for Walking This Road with Me

Share Your Story

Your Story Might Be the Reason Someone Else Begins

If our work together helped you feel more at home in your body, I would be honored if you shared a few words about it. Not for me, but for the person who is still searching. The one who is tired, discouraged, and wondering if anything can actually make sense of what they are feeling.

Your words could be the moment they realize they are not broken. They are simply waiting to be understood.

A review does not need to be long or polished. A sentence or two about where you started, what shifted, and how you feel now is more than enough.

Leave a Google Review

A public Google review helps others in our community find this work.

Email Your Experience to Lynette

Prefer something more personal? Send your story straight to Lynette. She reads every one.

Email Your Review

A small note: with your permission, your words may be shared on this site to encourage others. Nothing is ever posted without asking you first.

Not Sure What to Say?

Here are a few gentle prompts. Answer whichever ones feel true:

  • What were you feeling or carrying when you first reached out?
  • What shifted for you along the way?
  • What would you tell someone who is where you were?

You are not writing a report. You are leaving a light on for the next person.
