Can Stress Cause Tingling in Your Hands and Feet? What Your Nerves Are Saying

By Lynette Wing, Clinical Homeopath, Naturopath, Mind-Body Practitioner · October 25, 2026

← Back to the blog

Yes, stress and anxiety can cause tingling, numbness, or a pins-and-needles feeling, most often in the hands, feet, or face. Fast or shallow breathing during stress changes your blood chemistry, and the nerves notice. Tingling that is new, keeps coming back, or sits on one side of the body needs a physician's attention.

Why do my hands tingle when I am anxious?

When your nervous system senses danger, it speeds up your breathing so you can act. If you are not actually running from anything, you can end up breathing out more carbon dioxide than your body needs. That small shift in blood chemistry makes nerves more excitable, and many people feel it first as tingling in the fingers, lips, or around the mouth.

At the same time, stress pulls blood toward the heart and big muscles. Hands and feet can go cool and pale, and some people feel a prickly, half-asleep sensation along with the chill. I explain that side of the story in my post on whether stress can cause cold hands and feet.

I often see this in the people I sit with. A woman tells me her fingertips buzz every evening as she drives home from a hard job. She has been bracing all day, and her breath has been tight and high in her chest the whole time. I call symptoms messengers rather than enemies. Her tingling hands were not broken. They were telling her how long she had been holding her breath.

Can anxiety cause tingling all over the body?

Some people notice tingling that moves around: the scalp, the arms, the legs, or a strange electric feeling across the skin. When the alarm system stays switched on for weeks, the body can feel buzzy and sensitive much of the time. Tension in the neck and shoulders can also press on the nerves that run down into the arms.

The same wiring can show up as a tremble. If your hands both tingle and shake when you are nervous, my post on why stress can cause shaking walks through that side of the response.

What else can cause tingling in the hands and feet?

This matters. Tingling has many causes that have nothing to do with stress, including low vitamin B12, blood sugar problems, thyroid changes, pressure on a nerve in the wrist or neck, some medications, and other nerve conditions. The MedlinePlus page on numbness and tingling gives a plain-language overview of the many possibilities.

So please do not assume it is "just stress." Your physician can run simple tests and rule out what needs ruling out. Natural-first support works best alongside that care, never instead of it.

How can I calm tingling from anxiety?

Think of these as small signals of safety, offered alongside any care your physician recommends.

  1. Slow the out-breath. Breathe in gently through your nose for a count of four and out for a count of six. A longer exhale helps rebalance the breath that stress sped up.
  2. Breathe low. Rest one hand on your belly and let it rise under your hand. Breath that lives high in the chest feeds the tingling.
  3. Move your hands and feet. Wiggle your fingers and toes, make soft fists, and roll your ankles to bring warmth and circulation back.
  4. Loosen the shoulders. Roll them slowly, drop them away from your ears, and let your jaw soften.
  5. Warm up. A warm drink or warm water over your hands can help the body feel safe.
  6. Notice the pattern. Write down when it happens. That list helps you and your physician.

When should I see a doctor about tingling?

Please see your physician if tingling is new and does not go away, if it keeps coming back, if it spreads, or if it comes with weakness, clumsiness, or trouble walking.

Call 911 or seek emergency care right away for sudden numbness or weakness on one side of the face or body, a drooping face, trouble speaking, sudden confusion, or sudden vision changes. Also seek urgent care for tingling with chest pain or shortness of breath, or after a head, neck, or back injury.

FAQ

Can anxiety cause pins and needles?

Yes. Fast or shallow breathing during anxiety can cause pins and needles, often in the fingers, lips, or feet. Because tingling has many other causes, mention it to your physician.

Can stress cause numbness in one hand?

Stress-related tingling usually shows up on both sides. Numbness in one hand, or on one side of the body, should always be checked, and sudden one-sided numbness is an emergency.

Why do my feet tingle when I lie down at night?

When the day goes quiet, many people notice sensations they were too busy to feel. It can be the nervous system unwinding, but it can also come from circulation or nerve issues, so let your physician know.
